Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · Last updated: 9 August 2026 · Effective: 9 August 2026

This document is published in Turkish and English. For players in other languages, the English version governs.

This policy explains which personal data the mobile games developed by Yılkı Games (XOXD and the studio's other applications, together the “Game”) process, for which purposes and on which legal bases, to whom the data is disclosed, and what rights you have over it.

It also serves as the disclosure notice required by Article 10 of Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (“KVKK”). This policy applies when you download and use the Game; a summary is shown and your consent is requested the first time you open it.

2. Personal data we process

2.1. Account data

You need an account to use the online features. At that point we take your e-mail address and password. Your password is stored irreversibly hashed by our authentication provider; we cannot read it either.

We also store your chosen username, the account ID generated by the system, and appearance preferences such as avatar, background and name colour. Your username and appearance are visible to other players — we recommend not using your real name or anything that identifies you.

We do not ask for or store your real name, phone number, national ID number, address or date of birth.

2.2. Game data

  • Match history, moves and results
  • Points, rank and statistics from ranked matches
  • Win/loss/draw counters and streaks
  • Cosmetic items you own and achievement records
  • Your friend list and the friend requests you send and receive
  • Game invitations you send and receive
  • Your online status — while the app is open it periodically sends an “I'm here” signal to the server; if you have signalled within the last two minutes you appear online to your friends

2.3. Chat messages

Messages you send to friends are stored on our servers. They are not end-to-end encrypted. They pass through a profanity filter when displayed, but the raw text is kept on the server as written: if a user is reported, we need to see what was actually said. We do not analyse your messages for advertising or profiling.

2.4. Reports and blocks

When you report a player we record who reported whom, the reason chosen, a timestamp and any evidence snapshot captured at that moment (e.g. the relevant chat message).

When you block a player we record who blocked whom and when. Blocking takes effect immediately: the blocked account cannot send you messages, friend requests or game invites, and does not appear in your search results. You can see everyone you have blocked in Settings → Safety → Blocked Players and unblock at any time.

These records are used only to investigate misuse and to enforce the rules.

2.5. Purchase data

Premium purchases run entirely through Google Play. We do not see or store your card details. We only keep the expiry date of your membership and the Google Play purchase token used to verify it.

2.6. Technical and connection data

The server logs of our hosting and authentication provider may generate and briefly retain technical records such as IP address, request time and request type, for operating the service, debugging and preventing abuse. We do not use these for marketing and do not process them to build profiles.

2.7. Data that stays only on your device

Some data is never sent to the server. It stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • Your audio and language settings
  • Your level progress in challenge mode
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial
  • Your consent to this policy and the version you accepted
  • The authentication token that keeps you signed in
  • Your ad counter and the state of the discount offer

2.8. Advertising

The free version of the Game shows ads at intervals. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, AdMob may access your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), your IP address and general device information. We do not collect this data and have no access to it; the processing happens under Google's own privacy policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Personalised advertising depends on your explicit consent; if you are in a region where consent is required, a separate consent screen is shown when you first open the Game and you can change that choice later. Without consent, ads are served non-personalised and the Game keeps working. Premium members see no ads at all.

2.9. Data we do not process

  • We process no special categories of personal data (health, religion, political opinion, biometric data, etc.).
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar or the files on your device.
  • We use no analytics, measurement or user-tracking tools.
  • We do not collect crash logs or diagnostic data.

6.1. Deleting your account and data

To delete your account: send an e-mail titled “Account deletion request” to info@yilkigames.com from the address registered to your account, and include your in-game username.

We complete the request within 30 days and confirm when it is done. The action is irreversible.

When we delete your account, the following are permanently removed:

  • Your e-mail address and sign-in details
  • Your username, appearance preferences and statistics
  • Your match history
  • Your friend list, requests and chat messages
  • Your block records

Reports filed about you by other users, and purchase records we are legally required to keep, may be retained in a de-identified form until the periods above expire. Completed matches are anonymised so as not to break the other player's match history.

Note: uninstalling the app does not delete your account. If you have a subscription, you must also cancel it from your Google Play account.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

7.1. Rights under Article 11 of the KVKK

By applying to the data controller you may:

  1. Learn whether your personal data is processed,
  2. Request information if it has been processed,
  3. Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used accordingly,
  4. Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data is transferred,
  5. Request correction if the data is incomplete or inaccurate,
  6. Request erasure or destruction within the conditions of Article 7,
  7. Request that correction, erasure and destruction be notified to the third parties the data was transferred to,
  8. Object to a result adverse to you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems,
  9. Claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

7.2. How to apply

Send your application by e-mail to info@yilkigames.com. So that we can assess it, include your first and last name, the e-mail address registered to your account, your in-game username, the subject of your request, and an address at which we can reach you.

You may also apply by the other methods set out in the Communiqué on Application Procedures to the Data Controller (in writing, by secure electronic signature or by registered electronic mail).

7.3. Response time

We conclude your request as soon as possible and within thirty days at the latest, free of charge. If the request entails an additional cost, a fee set out in the tariff determined by the Board may be charged.

7.4. Complaint to the Board

If your application is rejected, if you find the response insufficient, or if no response is given in time, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board within thirty days of learning of the response and in any case within sixty days of the date of application (Article 14 of the KVKK).

7.5. EEA, Switzerland and the UK

If you are located in these regions, you also have the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21) and withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)) under the GDPR / UK GDPR. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

7.6. Automated decision-making

No solely automated decision-making or profiling producing legal effects concerning you, or similarly significantly affecting you, is carried out. Points and rank are calculated automatically, but that concerns only the operation of the game.

8. Children's privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from that age group. The Game is not part of Google Play's Families programme. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us data, we delete the account and its data. If your country sets a higher age for data processing, you may only use the Game with the permission of a parent or guardian.

9. Security and data breaches

All communication with the server is encrypted over HTTPS. The database enforces row-level access rules: the app cannot write to tables directly, and every operation goes through server-side functions whose privileges are checked. A player can only reach their own data and the data of matches they are in. Passwords are stored one-way hashed. Points and rank are calculated on the server only.

Even so, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute safety. We recommend a strong password that you do not use anywhere else.

If we determine that your personal data has been unlawfully obtained by others, we notify the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board and the affected users as soon as possible, in accordance with Article 12/5 of the KVKK.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. On update, the version number and date at the top of this page change. If a change is material (for example, if a new category of data begins to be processed), we ask for your consent again inside the Game. If you do not consent you cannot use the new feature; your existing account is not deleted automatically.

11. This website

The sections above concern our games. The site itself does far less: we use no cookies, there is no visitor tracking, no analytics and no advertising, and no requests are made to any third-party service.

The only exception: the language you pick is stored in your browser's own storage so you do not have to choose it again next time. That information is not sent to us and stays on your device. Which language the site opens in is read from your browser's own language preference — no external query is made for it.

Note: until 9 August 2026 the site asked an independent service for the visitor's country in order to pick a language, and the visitor's IP address reached that service in the process. This query has been removed entirely.
