Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026 · Effective: 7 August 2026

This document is published in Turkish and English. For players in other languages, this English version is the one that applies.

This policy explains what data the mobile games developed by Yılkı Games (XOXD and the studio's other applications, together the “Game”) collect, why we process it, who we share it with, and what rights you have over it.

By downloading and using the Game you accept this policy. A summary is shown to you the first time the Game launches, and your consent is recorded.

1. Data controller

Yılkı Games is the controller of your data. You can reach us about any privacy matter at info@yilkigames.com.

2. Data we collect

2.1. Account information

You need an account to use online features. For this we collect your email address and a password. Your password is stored irreversibly hashed by our authentication provider — we cannot see it either.

We also store your chosen username, avatar, background and name colour. Your username and appearance are visible to other players, so we recommend not using your real name or anything that identifies you.

2.2. Gameplay data

  • Match history, moves and results
  • Points, rank and statistics from ranked matches
  • Win / loss / draw counters and streaks
  • Your friend list and friend requests sent and received
  • Your online status — while the app is open it periodically sends a presence signal to the server; if you have signalled within the last two minutes you appear online to your friends

2.3. Chat messages

Messages you send to friends are stored on our servers. They are not end-to-end encrypted. A profanity filter is applied when messages are displayed, but the raw text is kept on the server as written: when a user is reported we need to see what was actually said. We do not analyse your messages for advertising or profiling.

2.4. Reports

When you report a player we record the reporting account, the reported account, the selected reason and a timestamp. These records are used only to investigate abuse and enforce our rules.

2.5. Purchase information

Premium purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card details. We keep only your subscription expiry date and the Google Play token used to verify the purchase.

2.6. Data that stays on your device

Some data never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the Game:

  • Your sound and language settings
  • Your progress in challenge mode
  • Your consent to this policy and the version you consented to
  • The authentication token that keeps you signed in

2.7. Advertising

The free version of the Game shows ads at intervals. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may access your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), IP address and general device information. We do not collect this data and have no access to it; the processing happens under Google's own privacy policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings. Premium members see no ads at all.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers the Game needs to run:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Your account and gameplay data are hosted there.
  • Google AdMob — ads in the free version.
  • Google Play Billing — premium membership payments.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to (a court order or a request from a competent authority).

Our providers' servers may be located outside Türkiye, in which case your data is transferred abroad.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and gameplay data: for as long as your account exists.
  • Chat messages: up to 12 months after sending.
  • Reports: up to 24 months after the case is closed, so we can see repeat offenders.
  • Purchase records: for the period required by tax law.

6. Deleting your account and data

To delete your account: send an email titled “Account deletion request” to info@yilkigames.com from the address registered to your account.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email once done.

When we delete your account, the following is permanently erased:

  • Your email address and sign-in credentials
  • Your username, appearance settings and statistics
  • Your friend list and chat messages

Reports filed against you by other users, and purchase records we are legally required to keep, may be retained in de-identified form until the periods above expire. Completed matches are anonymised rather than removed, so that your opponents' match history stays intact.

7. Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) and, if you are in the European Union, the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about you and request a copy
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing and withdraw consent you have given
  • Receive your data in a structured file (data portability)

To exercise any of these, email info@yilkigames.com. We respond to requests within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us data, we delete the account and the data. If you believe your child has provided us with data, please contact us.

9. Security

All communication with the server is encrypted over HTTPS. Our database enforces row-level access rules: the app cannot write to tables directly, and every operation goes through server functions that check permissions. A player can only reach their own data and the data of matches they are part of.

No system is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects you, we will notify you within the legally required time.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When a significant change is made we ask for your consent again inside the Game and update the date at the top of this page.
