Skyhand Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Effective: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Skyhand, a mobile game by Yılkı Games (“the Game”), handles your data. In short: Skyhand collects no personal data, sends nothing to our servers and contains no advertising or analytics. The Game runs entirely on your device.

1. Data controller

Erdem Korkmaz — Yılkı Games (sole proprietorship), İnönü Mahallesi, 4134/3 Sokak No: 5 Daire: 4, 35660 Menemen / İzmir, Türkiye · info@yilkigames.com

2. Data we collect

None. Skyhand does not ask you to create an account, and it does not request or read your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. There are no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools in the Game.

3. Data stored on your device

To work, the Game stores the following only on your own device, in the app's private folder. It is never sent to us or to third parties:

  • your world save (peoples, buildings, chronicle),
  • your settings (language, sound, whether you have seen the tutorial),
  • whether you own the Skyhand Full Pack (so it stays unlocked while offline).

Uninstalling the Game deletes all of this data from your device.

4. In-app purchase

The Skyhand Full Pack is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment and your payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card or account details. The purchase is tied to your Google account, and the Game asks Google Play on your device whether you own it. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Sharing and transfers

Because we collect no personal data, we do not share or transfer any data to third parties or other countries.

6. Children's privacy

Skyhand is not directed at children under 13. No personal data is collected from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

To exercise your rights under the GDPR, the Turkish data protection law (KVKK) or similar laws, or to ask a question, write to info@yilkigames.com. As we hold no data about you, uninstalling the Game is enough to delete the data on your device.

8. Changes

If the Game ever gains a feature that collects data, this page will be updated beforehand and the change announced in the Game.
